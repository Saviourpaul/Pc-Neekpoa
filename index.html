About Me

"I craft strategic copy and compelling storytelling that works"

I'm Patrick Clement Neekpoa, popularly known as PC Neekpoa. I'm a Nigerian digital creator, content writer, graphic designer, AI data annotator, and social media manager passionate about storytelling and creative communication. Over the years, I've worked across sports and entertainment media, content creation, digital branding, and AI-related projects. My experience has helped me develop a unique blend of creativity, strategy, and technology, allowing me to create content that informs, engages, and inspires. Whether I'm writing, designing, managing social media, or contributing to AI training projects, I approach every task with curiosity, professionalism, and a commitment to excellence. I believe powerful stories and meaningful content can build connections, strengthen brands, and leave a lasting impact; and that's what I strive to deliver through my work.
